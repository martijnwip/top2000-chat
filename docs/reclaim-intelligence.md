## Why should we reclaim intelligence?

It means flipping the script: AI that works for you, not the other way around. It means building AI for how the world actually needs it today.

1. **Your data should stay yours** — because privacy is a right, not a feature to negotiate.
2. **AI should be explainable** — because you control your tools when you understand them.
3. **Infrastructure should be sustainable** — because the future of AI shouldn't cost us the planet.
4. **AI should serve human intelligence** — because it's a tool that enhances, not replaces, human understanding.
5. **AI should encourage curiosity** — because the best tools evolve with you, fostering exploration and continuous learning.

---

## AI is a tool. Not a companion.

Genuine empathy cannot be simulated. It must be nurtured and protected.

1. **Empathy belongs to humans.** AI processes information. It does not "understand," "feel," or "empathize." These capacities belong to humans alone.
2. **Technology that protects connection.** We build AI that safeguards authentic human relationships, not systems that pretend to replace them.
3. **Designed for trusted use.** AI that integrates into daily experiences as a reliable tool — not a substitute for human judgment.
